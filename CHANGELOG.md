# Changelog

## v2.0.0 (2026-11-05)

V2 of [Christian Trivia](https://github.com/maumoya/christian-trivia): from a scene-by-scene trivia game to an
adventure you walk through.

- Overworld for Chapter 1, Creation: walk between the seven days, reach the glowing spots, answer one question per scene.
- Quests, timeline cards, story items, verse pieces, badges and a companion.
- English and Español, with read-aloud. Verses: WEB and KJV (English), RVR1909 (Spanish).
- Two players on one keyboard or screen.
- Grown-ups area behind an adult check: daily minutes, quiet hours, chapters per session.
- Offline PWA: every file is cached on the first visit. No accounts, analytics or third-party hosts.
- 40 automated tests (`node --test`), no dependencies.
