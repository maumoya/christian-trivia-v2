# Christian Trivia V2 (ARCA: Creation)

**Owner and sole author:** Mauricio Alonso Moya Tapia. Code licensed under MIT (see `LICENSE`).

An offline Bible adventure for children aged 5 to 10: Chapter 1, Creation. V2 of
[Christian Trivia](https://github.com/maumoya/christian-trivia).

- Bible texts: KJV, WEB and RVR1909 (public domain), from eBible.org. Sources are in each file.
- Art: original, drawn in code. No third-party art, fonts or scripts.
- Data: nothing leaves the device. No accounts, analytics, ads or purchases.
